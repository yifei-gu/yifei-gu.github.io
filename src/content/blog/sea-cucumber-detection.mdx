# Detecting Sea Cucumbers with Explainable AI

Deposit-feeding sea cucumbers recycle nutrients, but counting them over large areas is hard. Occlusion, low light, and turbid water all make a detector trained in one place fail somewhere else.

## What we built

We used sea cucumbers as the example species for a YOLOv8 survey pipeline. Images came from a GoPro Hero9 Black under a range of turbid conditions. Photometric augmentation was aimed at small datasets, and it is what let the model generalize to sites that were held out of training. Eigen-CAM (an eigenvalue class activation map) draws the perceptual field on those unseen frames so a biologist can see whether the box is sitting on the animal.

## Result

The detection model reached 94.1% mean average precision. The augmentation step is what carried that accuracy onto unseen sites despite the limited training set.

Code, including the Eigen-CAM examples, is in [holothurian-YOLOv8](https://github.com/Vicellken/holothurian-YOLOv8). It is a conda and Ultralytics project, not a packaged `pip install`.
